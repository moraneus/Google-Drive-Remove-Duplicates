4 · Clean

Files are moved to Drive's trash (recoverable for 30 days). An audit log is kept locally, and you can undo the last batch here.

Past batches & freeing storage

⚠️ Files in Drive's trash still count toward your storage quota until the trash is emptied. "Delete permanently" empties just the files of one batch — only those still in the trash — and cannot be undone.

5 · Empty folders

Folders with no files anywhere inside (folders that only contain other empty folders count too). Trashing a folder also removes its empty subfolders.

6 · Older files

Find files you haven't touched in years. These are unique files (not duplicates), so nothing is pre-selected — tick only what you're sure about.

7 · Largest files

Your biggest files, largest first — the quickest way to free space when Drive is full. Nothing is pre-selected.
